01 Executive Summary & Responsive Layout

Core Architecture

The KKK Oil Factory Enterprise ERP provides complete end-to-end digitisation for edible oil extraction mills and commercial distribution. The application operates simultaneously on desktop workstations and handheld mobile devices using an adaptive responsive shell.

Desktop vs. Mobile Experience

  • Desktop Workstations (≥ 860px): Features a permanent 244px dark sidebar with gold accents, top bar with global search and notification bells, and wide data tables for efficient keyboard-driven data entry.
  • Mobile Smart Devices (< 860px): Provides a streamlined 5-station bottom navigation bar (Dashboard, Billing, Inventory, History, and More Modules bottom sheet) with touch-friendly buttons and mobile thermal printer integration.
  • Force Desktop View Toggle: On tablet devices or mobile screens, tapping the topbar Desktop View button forces full desktop table mode, enabling viewing of complex multi-column billing sheets.

The central enterprise billing engine for wholesale orders, dealers, distributors, supermarkets, and institutions. Formats tax invoices strictly conforming to Rule 46 of CGST Rules, 2017.

Header Form Controls

  • Customer Selector: Searchable dropdown displaying customer legal name, GSTIN, and current balance.
  • Route: Target delivery corridor (e.g. Dharmapuri Local, Salem Highway, Coimbatore Express).
  • Godown Selector: Deducts stock from Main Godown, Packaging Godown, or Mill Floor.
  • Pricing Tier: Radio buttons for Retail Rate, Semi-Wholesale Rate, or Bulk Wholesale Rate.
  • Payment Mode: Dropdown options: Cash, Credit / Account, UPI / QR, Bank Transfer, Cheque.
  • Payment Status: Paid, Partial, or Credit (Unpaid).

Line Item Entry & Automation

  • Item Search Autocomplete: Type SKU code or name; automatically fetches HSN, packaging unit, and rate.
  • Quantity: Number of units to add.
  • Applied Unit Rate: Auto-populated from chosen pricing tier; editable by authorized managers.
  • Discount %: Line discount percentage (e.g., 2.0%).
  • + Add to Voucher: Inserts product into line items table with automatic tax calculations.

Automated GST Tax Engine

The system compares the customer's 2-digit GST state code with the factory code (Tamil Nadu: 33):

  • Intra-State Sale (Same State: 33): Applies CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5% = 5.0% Total Tax.
  • Inter-State Sale (Out of State: e.g. 29, 32): Applies IGST 5.0%.

Bottom Multi-Tab Panel

  • Tab 0 (Dispatch Details): Input Buyer PO Number, PO Date, Vehicle Number (e.g. TN 28 BK 5521), Driver Name, and Delivery Challan No.
  • Tab 1 (E-Way Bill): Mandatory 12-digit E-Way Bill Number and validity duration for shipments over ₹50,000.
  • Tab 2 (E-Invoice): 64-character IRN hash, QR code data, and Government portal acknowledgement.
  • Tab 3 (Tax Ledgers): Comprehensive ledger breakdown showing pre-tax subtotal, CGST, SGST, IGST, round-off, and grand total.

Action Buttons

  • Save Invoice: Validates all inputs, deducts godown stock, updates customer credit ledger, creates permanent voucher ID (V-2026-XXXX), and saves to Firestore.
  • Print A4 Tax Invoice: Generates statutory 300 DPI A4 PDF invoice complete with bank details and signature block.
  • Print Thermal Receipt: Emits 80mm / 58mm ESC/POS slip.
  • Clear Form: Resets all fields for next bill.

21 Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Operational Workflows

SOP 1: Cashier Shift Opening & POS Counter Billing

  1. Sign in at /login using cashier credentials (cashier).
  2. System opens /counter automatically. Count and verify cash drawer opening float.
  3. For walk-in customers, tap product icons to populate cart. Adjust quantities using + / - steppers.
  4. Select payment mode: Cash or UPI QR.
  5. If cash, type customer amount into Cash Received; confirm Change to Return.
  6. Click Complete & Print Thermal. Hand receipt and oil to customer.
  7. At end of shift, review /billing-history, verify cash against system totals, and hand over physical cash bag to Manager.

SOP 2: Creating a B2B Wholesale GST Tax Invoice

  1. Navigate to /billing.
  2. Select customer from Customer Selector. Confirm displayed credit balance.
  3. Verify dispatch godown (Main Godown) and delivery route.
  4. Select price tier: Bulk Wholesale Rate.
  5. Search SKU (e.g. PRD-GN-15L), enter quantity (e.g. 50 tins), and click + Add to Voucher.
  6. Confirm automated CGST/SGST (intra-state) or IGST (inter-state) tax computation.
  7. Click Bottom Tab 0 (Dispatch); enter Vehicle Number, Driver Name, and Buyer PO Number.
  8. If invoice exceeds ₹50,000, enter E-Way Bill Number in Tab 1.
  9. Click Save Invoice, then click Print A4 Tax Invoice. Print original and transporter copies.

SOP 3: Mandi Seed Intake & Weighbridge Recording

  1. Weigh loaded seed truck on weighbridge to obtain Gross Weight.
  2. QC technician inspects seed sample for moisture content and kernel damage.
  3. Navigate to /purchase and click + Record Seed Inward.
  4. Select vendor, enter material (Groundnut Raw Pods), net weight (Gross - Tare), and rate per kg.
  5. Set QC Status: Passed (Graded) or Pending Lab Test.
  6. Click Save Inward Entry to generate Goods Receipt Note (GRN) and update supplier payable ledger.

SOP 4: Expeller Milling Run & Yield Calculation

  1. Allocate raw seeds (e.g. 5,000 kg groundnut pods) from warehouse to expeller batteries.
  2. After crushing and filtration, measure recovered oil volume in settling tank.
  3. Navigate to /production and click + Start New Production Milling Run.
  4. Enter Seed Input (5,000 kg), Filtered Oil Output (e.g. 2,150 Liters), and Bagged Cake Output (2,850 kg).
  5. Verify extraction yield (40% - 43%) and click Record Milling Run. Bulk oil volume automatically updates in /inventory.
