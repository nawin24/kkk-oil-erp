01 Executive Overview & Business Architecture

Enterprise Domain

The KKK Oil Factory ERP & Billing System is a purpose-built industrial management platform designed for modern edible oil manufacturing facilities. The platform automates the complete lifecycle of oilseed processing—from raw seed procurement at agricultural mandis through multi-stage expeller extraction, settling, packaging, and commercial tax-compliant distribution.

Core Industry Challenges Solved

  • Commodity Volatility: Edible oil raw materials (groundnut kernels, sesame seeds, mustard seeds, coconuts) fluctuate daily based on regional mandi arrivals. The system tracks raw material costs in real-time against retail and wholesale selling rates.
  • Complex Processing Yields: Oil expelling inherently produces finished oil alongside valuable byproducts (oil cake / de-oiled cake) and processing moisture loss. The production module continuously validates physical mass balance.
  • Statutory GST Compliance: Seamless generation of Tax Invoices with accurate HSN classification (HSN 1515 for vegetable oils, HSN 2306 for oil cake) and multi-state CGST/SGST/IGST tax logic.
  • Dual Counter Operations: Supports high-speed walk-in retail counter billing alongside structured B2B wholesale orders with customer credit limits and delivery challans.

02 Technical Architecture & State Management

Frontend & Data Pipeline

Built on Flutter 3.x and Dart 3.x, the application delivers a unified codebase operating natively on Web browsers, Android hand-held tablets/smartphones, iOS devices, and desktop environments.

Architecture Highlights

  • Reactive State Management: Powered by package:provider. AuthProvider handles user credentials, cryptographic password hashing, role permissions, and active session tokens. DataProvider maintains real-time operational state including products, batches, stock levels, orders, and customer accounts.
  • Session Persistence: Implemented via StorageService utilizing SharedPreferences. User login state, active stations (GST vs. Non-GST), and profile metadata persist across full browser refreshes and direct URL changes without kickouts.
  • HTML5 Clean URL Routing: Standardized path routing (e.g., /dashboard, /billing, /products, /inventory, /production) enabled via custom URL strategy without legacy /#/ fragments.
  • Resilient Offline-First Synchronization: Google Cloud Firestore handles cloud synchronization with local disk caching for continuous factory operations during intermittent network drops.

04 Dual Billing Engines (Counter POS & GST B2B)

Billing & Tax Engine

The system features two specialized billing workflows designed for distinct operational velocities:

1. Quick Counter POS (`/counter`)

  • Optimized for retail counter staff serving retail customers.
  • One-tap SKU selection with automatic tax inclusion.
  • Tendered cash calculation showing exact change returnable to the customer.
  • Generates 80mm or 58mm thermal receipts with store logo, receipt number, date/time, and tax summary.

2. Enterprise B2B GST Invoicing (`/billing`)

  • HSN Code Management: Pre-configured HSN 1515 (5% GST for edible oil) and HSN 2306 (5% GST for oil cake byproducts).
  • Automatic Tax Split: Evaluates customer's state code vs. Company state code (33 - Tamil Nadu):
    • Intra-State (Tamil Nadu): CGST (2.5%) + SGST (2.5%)
    • Inter-State (Other States): IGST (5.0%)
  • PDF Invoicing Service: Generates formal A4 invoices featuring company branding, bank details (IFSC, Account Number), UPI payment QR code, transport vehicle number, and customer GSTIN.

05 Expeller Crushing & Production Tracking

Factory Operations

Accurately monitors the conversion of raw agricultural seeds into filtered oil and cake byproducts:

Mass Balance Formulas:

Oil Yield % = (Filtered Oil Output [kg] / Raw Seed Input [kg]) × 100
Cake Yield % = (Oil Cake Produced [kg] / Raw Seed Input [kg]) × 100
Processing Loss % = 100% - (Oil Yield % + Cake Yield %)

Standard Operating Benchmark (Groundnut Expeller Run)

  • Raw Seed Input: 1,000 kg Groundnut Pods (Kernels)
  • Expected Oil Recovery: ~400 kg to 420 kg (40% - 42% yield)
  • Expected Oil Cake Byproduct: ~540 kg to 560 kg (54% - 56% yield)
  • Normal Evaporation & Moisture Loss: ~20 kg to 40 kg (2% - 4%)

06 Inventory, Silo Tanks & Packaged SKUs

Warehouse Management

Manages raw materials, intermediate storage, and finished goods across multiple warehouse locations:

  • Bulk Silo Tanks: Tracks dip stick readings (centimeters) and calculates volume using tank geometry formulas.
  • Packaged Retail SKUs: Tracks 15 kg Tins, 15L Tins, 5L Cans, 1L PET Bottles, and 500ml Pouches.
  • Byproducts: Manages 50 kg gunny bags of groundnut cake, sesame cake, and cotton seed cake sold to dairy farms.
  • Packaging Supplies: Monitors raw tin containers, HDPE bottles, plastic caps, cartons, and adhesive brand labels.

07 Procurement, Mandi Purchasing & Vendors

Supply Chain

Facilitates direct procurement from agricultural farmers, mandi commission agents, and commodity brokers:

  • Weighbridge Integration: Records Gross Truck Weight, Tare Weight, and Net Weight with bag counts.
  • Quality Parameter Deductions: Applies deduction factors for excess seed moisture percentage, foreign material, and damaged kernels.
  • Vendor Ledgers: Automated generation of Purchase Invoices, tracking accounts payable, payment history, and credit periods.

08 Logistics, Dispatch & E-Way Bill Workflow

Distribution

Manages the movement of goods from factory loading docks to dealer stores and customer warehouses:

  • Dispatch Run-Sheets: Groups pending sales orders by delivery routes and vehicle capacity.
  • E-Way Bill Recording: Stores the 12-digit Government E-Way Bill Number and transporter vehicle registration number for consignments over Rs. 50,000.
  • Live Delivery Status: Tracks consignment progression through Pending, Loaded, In Transit, and Delivered milestones.

09 Reports, Daybook & Tax Compliance

Financial Auditing

Provides executive decision-makers with comprehensive accounting and tax compliance reports:

  • Daily Daybook: Real-time consolidation of daily cash counter intake, bank deposits, credit sales, and vendor payments.
  • GSTR-1 Tax Summaries: Summarizes B2B invoices (Table 4), B2C supplies (Table 5/7), and HSN summary (Table 12) for monthly GST filing.
  • Inventory Valuation: Computes total warehouse asset value using standard weighted-average valuation.

Standard Operating Procedure: Shift Closing Checklist

  1. Confirm all counter orders are marked as Paid or Credit.
  2. Navigate to Reports -> Daybook.
  3. Perform physical cash count and verify against system cash total.
  4. Verify UPI digital receipts against bank account notification ledger.
  5. Super Admin or Manager signs off on shift closing report.
