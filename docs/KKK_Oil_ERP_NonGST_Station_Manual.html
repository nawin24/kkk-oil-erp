Statutory Firewall Guarantee

Under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, transactions created within the Dedicated Non-GST Station are completely excluded from GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, e-Way bill portals, and formal tax liability computations. The system enforces cryptographic isolation between commercial tax invoices and local agricultural trade.

1. Agricultural Mandi Procurement

Local smallholder farmers selling groundnut pods, sesame seeds, and copra directly at factory gates are exempt from mandatory GST registration. The Non-GST Station allows logging physical weighbridge arrivals, net weights, and instant cash settlements without generating statutory tax invoices.

2. Unbranded Loose Oil Sales

Village retail customers purchasing unbranded edible oil dispensed into their own containers at the mill gate require rapid cash receipts. The Non-GST counter prints 0% tax delivery memos without requiring buyer GSTIN or statutory E-invoice IRN numbers.

3. Private Godown Inventory Isolation

All Non-GST vouchers automatically deduct quantities from Private Godown or Local Yard. Statutory warehouses (Main Godown, Packaging Depot) are untouchable from this station, preventing audited stock discrepancies.

4. Dual Customer Credit Architecture

Dealers purchasing both statutory tax-invoiced packaged goods and non-tax raw seeds maintain two distinct ledgers:
• GST Credit Balance (Subject to legal tax statements).
• Non-GST Balance (Private mandi trading ledger).

Dual Customer Credit Balance Architecture

Commercial dealers frequently engage in two distinct streams of commerce with KKK Oil Mill:

1. Statutory GST Credit Ledger

Debited by GST-XXXX invoices. Formally reported on monthly buyer statements and subject to legal recovery and audited accounts.

2. Private Non-GST Credit Ledger

Debited exclusively by NG-XXXX vouchers. Tracks informal mandi trade, loose oil barrels, and local cash settlements privately.

Private Godown Inventory Isolation

Inventory is partitioned into two air-gapped physical and digital domains:

Warehouse Domain Accessible From Audit Impact
Main Godown & Packaging GST Billing (/billing) Subject to physical auditor inspection & GSTR-9 balance sheets.
Private Godown & Mandi Yard Non-GST Billing (/nongst-billing) Excluded from statutory stock valuation. Tracks raw farm lots.
Safeguard: The system strictly prohibits selecting the Main Godown while in Non-GST Billing mode.

SOP NG-1: Mandi Smallholder Farmer Settlement

  1. Verify farmer agricultural exemption under GST Notification No. 12/2017.
  2. Record net seed weight at weighbridge and route inward to Private Godown.
  3. Create settlement voucher in Non-GST station with zero tax.
  4. Disburse cash or rural account transfer. Hand over delivery memo.

SOP NG-2: Mill Gate Loose Oil Dispensing

  1. Tare customer's container on electronic scale.
  2. Fill requested volume (e.g. 5kg unbranded groundnut oil).
  3. Select Walk-in / Cash Customer on mobile POS. Press Save & Print.
  4. Hand over thermal delivery receipt with hard-coded 0% tax.
Statutory Compliance Confirmation: The mill's chartered accountant only accesses standard GST reports. Non-GST records remain completely isolated from tax audits.
