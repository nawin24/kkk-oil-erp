The Dedicated Non-GST Billing Station provides an isolated environment for managing physical trade transactions that operate outside the statutory GST tax regime under Indian agricultural marketing guidelines.

Core Use Cases

  • Agricultural Mandi Procurement: Small, unregistered farmers selling raw groundnut pods, sesame seeds, and copra directly to the mill are legally exempt from GST registration. The Non-GST station records physical net weights and cash disbursements accurately without generating tax invoices.
  • Unbranded Loose Oil Counter Sales: Local customers purchasing oil in their own containers or unbranded cans at factory mill gates require fast cash receipts without buyer GSTIN entry.
  • Private Holding Warehouse Stock: Unprocessed seed lots held in private godowns prior to formal cleaning and commercial grading are tracked independently of statutory inventory.
  • Absolute Statutory Isolation: All transactions in this station carry the NG- prefix, lock the tax rate strictly to 0.0%, and are completely quarantined from GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and statutory audited financials.

The primary voucher creation interface for non-tax commercial trade, cash sales, and mandi farmer settlements.

Header Form Controls

  • Customer Selector: Searchable dropdown displaying buyer name and their specific Non-GST Outstanding Balance.
  • Billing Route: Target delivery corridor (e.g., Dharmapuri Local, Salem Highway, Namakkal Mandi).
  • Godown Selector: Defaults to Private Godown (or Local Mandi Yard). Protects statutory tax-audited inventory.
  • Pricing Tier: Radio buttons for Retail Rate, Semi-Wholesale Rate, or Bulk Wholesale Rate.
  • Payment Mode: Cash, Credit / Account, UPI / QR.
  • Payment Status: Paid, Partial, or Credit (Unpaid).

Line Item Entry & Strict 0.0% GST Engine

  • Item Search Autocomplete: Fast SKU search by code or name.
  • Quantity & Rate: Enter units and rate per unit (₹).
  • Discount %: Line discount percentage (if negotiated).
  • + Add to Voucher: Inserts product into table with zero-tax calculation.

Calculation Rules

  • $ ext{Net Line Amount} = ( ext{Qty} imes ext{Rate}) - ext{Discount}$
  • $ ext{GST Rate} = 0.0\%$ (Strictly locked)
  • $ ext{GST Amount} = ₹0.00$
  • $ ext{Taxable Amount} = ext{Net Line Amount}$

Action Buttons

  • Save Voucher: Validates inputs, creates voucher with permanent prefix NG-2026-XXXX, deducts stock from Private Godown, and updates customer Non-GST ledger.
  • Print A4 Delivery Voucher: Generates formal A4 delivery memo / estimate PDF without GSTIN or statutory tax declarations.
  • Print Thermal Receipt: Emits 80mm / 58mm ESC/POS receipt for immediate handover.
  • Clear Form: Wipes form fields for next transaction.

07 Private Godown Stock Isolation

Inventory Segregation

Physical inventory is separated between statutory tax-audited stock and private operational stock:

  • Main Godown & Packaging Godown: Official inventory tracked in statutory tax registers and audited in balance sheets. Deducted exclusively by standard /billing invoices.
  • Private Godown & Mandi Yard: Dedicated storage for uncleaned seed intake from local farmers and loose oil tanks. Deducted exclusively by /nongst-billing vouchers.
  • Cancellation Restoration: Cancelling an NG- voucher returns items strictly to Private Godown, ensuring official inventory numbers are never altered.

08 Step-by-Step Non-GST Workflows

Standard Operating Procedures

SOP 1: Issuing a Non-GST Delivery Voucher

  1. Sign in as admin (Super Admin). Navigate to /nongst-billing.
  2. Verify the top amber alert banner: *"NON-GST BILLING STATION — All tax rates are locked to 0.0%..."*
  3. Select buyer from Customer Selector. Confirm their displayed Non-GST credit balance.
  4. Ensure Godown is set to Private Godown.
  5. In Item Search, type SKU name; enter quantity and rate; click + Add to Voucher.
  6. Confirm in items table: GST Rate is 0.0% and GST Amount is ₹0.00.
  7. Click bottom tab Dispatch Details; enter vehicle number and driver name.
  8. Click Save Voucher. System creates NG-2026-XXXX and deducts Private Godown stock.
  9. Click Print A4 Delivery Voucher or Print Thermal Receipt.

SOP 2: Cancelling a Non-GST Voucher

  1. Navigate to /nongst-history.
  2. Search for voucher number (e.g. NG-2026-0015).
  3. Click red Cancel Voucher button.
  4. Enter staff cancellation reason in dialog.
  5. Click Confirm Cancellation. System restores quantities to Private Godown and reverses customer debit.

10 Audit Compliance & Safeguards

Statutory Protection
CRITICAL COMPLIANCE RULES:
  1. Never use the Non-GST station for registered B2B dealers who require statutory GST input tax credit (ITC).
  2. Official monthly tax filings at /reports automatically exclude NG- vouchers. Never manually merge non-tax registers into statutory accounting files.
  3. Reconcile physical cash from Non-GST counter sales into a dedicated, separate cash drawer box.
  4. Conduct periodic physical stock audits of Private Godowns separately from Main Warehouse audits.
