OPERATIONAL WORKFLOWS Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
FACTORY WORKFLOWS SOP 1 – SOP 4

SOP 1: Morning Mill Startup & Tank Dip Check

  1. 07:30 AM — Tank Inspection: Warehouse incharge conducts physical dip-stick measurement across Crude Silo, Filtered Silo, and Settling Tanks.
  2. Log Readings: Open /inventory and click Record Tank Dip. Input depth in centimeters; system auto-computes volume in litres.
  3. Review Safety Buffers: Verify that finished packaged stock (1L bottles and 15L tins) meets the morning distributor dispatch schedule.
  4. Expeller Inspection: Production supervisor confirms seed hopper levels and filter press cloth cleanliness.

SOP 2: High-Speed Counter Cashier Shift

  1. 08:00 AM — Sign In: Cashier logs into workstation or mobile POS using individual credentials (cashier).
  2. Open POS Counter: Navigate to /counter. Confirm initial cash drawer float (e.g. ₹2,000 in small denominations).
  3. Scan & Tap Sale: Tap product tiles or scan barcode. Tap price tier (Retail / Wholesale). Select payment mode (Cash/UPI).
  4. Thermal Slip Handover: System automatically triggers 80mm ESC/POS slip. Tender change promptly.

SOP 3: B2B Wholesale Billing & Vehicle Dispatch

  1. Customer Search: Open /billing. Select registered dealer. Review GSTIN and current credit balance.
  2. Price Tier & Godown: Select Agency Rate or Wholesale Rate. Select deducting warehouse (Main Godown).
  3. Line Items & Tax Verification: Add products. System auto-verifies intra-state (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%) or inter-state (IGST 5.0%).
  4. Dispatch Details & E-Way Bill: Fill PO Number, Vehicle Number (e.g. TN 28 BK 5521), and 12-digit E-Way Bill if bill > ₹50,000.
  5. Commit & Print: Press F2 to save. Print 300 DPI A4 Tax Invoice (Triplicate: Original for Buyer, Duplicate for Transporter, Triplicate for Mill).

SOP 4: Evening Cashier Reconciliation & Day Closing

  1. Count Physical Cash: Cashier counts all currency notes and coins in physical register drawer.
  2. Cross-Check System Total: Open /dashboard. Compare drawer cash count against Cash Register Balance KPI card.
  3. Reconcile UPI & Bank Transfers: Match digital UPI transaction references against daily bank settlement report.
  4. Manager Shift Handover: Plant manager approves daily cashier reconciliation slip and locks shift records.
  5. Automated Cloud Sync: System ensures all offline vouchers are securely committed to Firestore cloud storage.
